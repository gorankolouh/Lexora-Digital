What we do

Bodywork done properly.

Every job gets the same treatment: strip it back, fix it right, prime, paint and polish until you can't see where the damage was.

01

Crash repair

Accident damage repaired from the frame out: panels straightened or replaced, lines put back exactly where they should be.

02

Resprays

Single panels or full resprays in our booth, colour-matched and blended so new paint sits invisibly next to the old.

03

Dents & scratches

Car-park knocks, keyed doors and kerb scrapes filled, flatted and refinished to a smooth, factory-looking surface.

04

Bumper repairs

Cracked, split or scuffed bumpers repaired and refinished. Often cheaper than a new one, and just as good.

05

Diamond cut alloys

Kerbed or corroded alloys stripped, painted and diamond cut on the lathe for that bright two-tone finish.

06

Colour matching

Solid, metallic or pearl, mixed to your car's paint code and checked in daylight before it goes on.
