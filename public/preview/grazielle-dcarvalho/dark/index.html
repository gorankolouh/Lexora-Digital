Services

The hair you want, made to blend.

Every appointment starts with a look at your own hair: its thickness, colour and condition. Then we choose the method that will look most natural and last best for you.

Handpicked hair
Fitted by hand
  1. 01

    Tape-in extensions

    Thin, flat tapes that sit close to the scalp. Quick to fit, comfortable to wear and ideal for adding length and fullness to fine hair.

    Lightweight · Quick fitting
  2. 02

    Keratin bonds

    Strand-by-strand bonds attached with keratin for the most discreet, natural movement. Wear your hair up with confidence.

    K-tip · Strand by strand
  3. 03

    Micro rings

    Small rings that hold each strand without heat or glue. Gentle on your hair and easy to adjust at every maintenance visit.

    No heat · No glue
  4. 04

    Weft extensions

    Rows of hair for big volume and dramatic length. A great choice for thicker hair or a full transformation in one visit.

    Volume · Full transformation
  5. 05

    Maintenance, move-ups & removal

    Regular refits keep your extensions fresh and your own hair healthy. Safe, careful removal whenever you are ready for a break.

    Refit · Move-up · Removal
  6. 06

    Colour match, cut & blend

    We match the shade to your own hair, then cut and style everything together for a seamless finish from root to tip.

    Colour match · Blending · Styling

About
